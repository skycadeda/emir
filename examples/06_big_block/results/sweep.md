# RCC against CC + cc-grid (Xyce, synthetic, serial)

Generated by `examples/06_big_block/sweep.py`. An invented N x N mesh (0.25 ohm segments, 2 fF per node) fed at one corner, with level-1 CMOS inverters switching at 1 GHz in eight phases, 4 ns simulated. Not a real design, not Spectre: an illustration of how the two routes scale. Times are wall seconds on a shared machine with 24 hardware threads, single-threaded; `load` is its 1-minute load average when the row's simulations started (other work ran too), so read ratios, not absolute times.
